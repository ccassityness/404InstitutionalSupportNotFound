404: Institutional Support Not Found
Cailey Cassity-Ness, 2025


This web-based electronic literature project
transforms the 404 error
— a symbol of digital failure —
into a poetic archive
of institutional abandonment.

Through a series of error pages,
this work documents the material consequences
of federal research defunding
on digital humanities labs,
projects,
and communities.

Each 404 page functions as both
        memorial
        and critique,

replacing "page not found" messages
with concrete poetry that reveals
what happens when

        grants expire,
        servers are decommissioned,
        and institutional support evaporates.


This work draws on actual examples
of defunded Digital Humanities projects and labs,
transforming higher education administrative language
into found poetry
that makes visible the human cost of
"strategic priorities"
and "budget realities."


This project exists as
a deliberately broken website
where every navigation choice leads
to a different 404 poem,
each documenting a specific type of institutional loss:

        expired grants
        eliminated positions
        deleted student work
        closed labs
        orphaned community partnerships
        decommissioned servers


Visitors encounter the same infrastructural precarity
that actual Digital Humanities practitioners face,
experiencing firsthand
how institutional support doesn't just fade:

        it 404s.





Keywords: 404 errors, federal defunding, digital humanities,
found poetry, web-based literature, electronic literature,
institutional critique
