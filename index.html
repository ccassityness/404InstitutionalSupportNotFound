The Center for Digital Humanities Research supports interdisciplinary scholarship at the intersection of technology and the humanities. We collaborate with faculty, students, and community partners to build enduring digital projects.

Current Projects

Regional Oral History Archive

Funded 2021–2024 · NEH Preservation & Access

A community-sourced audio archive documenting three generations of labor organizing in the post-industrial Midwest. 847 interviews. 1,200+ hours.

Indigenous Language Revitalization Network

In partnership with seven tribal colleges

Collaborative platform for language teachers, learners, and elders. Open-access pedagogy tools and archival integration.

Mapping Redlining: A Historical GIS

NSF Grant #2114882 · Graduate Research Fellowship

Georeferenced HOLC maps linked to census records, property deeds, and oral testimony across 46 cities.

Pandemic Pedagogy Archive

IMLS Laura Bush 21st Century Librarian Program

Faculty and student accounts of emergency remote teaching, 2020–2022. Preserved in partnership with University Libraries.

Queer Zine Digitization Initiative

Mellon Foundation · In progress

Full-text searchable archive of 4,000+ zines from LGBTQ+ community collections. Student digitization practicum.
