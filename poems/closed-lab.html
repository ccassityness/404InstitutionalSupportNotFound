III. Student Comment, End-of-Semester Survey, Spring 2025

Q: Is there anything else you'd like us to know
   about your experience in the DH Lab this semester?

A: I took the long way to class every day
   just so I could walk past the lab.
   I don't know why.
   I guess I liked knowing it was there.

   I'm going to miss the sign-in sheet.
   I know that sounds weird.

   I'm going to miss signing my name
   next to people I didn't know
   who were working on things I didn't understand
   in a room that was always
   a little too warm
   and never quiet enough
   and exactly right.
